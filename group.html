About my group

My group will develop theoretically grounded machine-learning methods for scientific applications. We welcome students from mathematical, computational, and other scientific backgrounds (chemistry and brain imaging in particular) who care about writing clear mathematics and clean code.

Here are some research directions I am currently interested in:

  • diffusion models for text data: discrete token spaces, variable-length sequences, and product spaces combining text with other modalities
  • sampling algorithms for molecular data: scalable methods for multimodal landscapes and representations that aid sample generation
  • causal discovery algorithms for brain imaging data: methods that scale across subjects and help characterize stimulus-response pathways in dynamical systems
  • theory for when machine learning algorithms scale: sample or computational complexity as a function of design choices
