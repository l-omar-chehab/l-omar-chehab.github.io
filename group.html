My group is launching at UW-Madison in Fall 2026!

If you are a student who shares some of my research interests, has some experience in these areas or other areas, or think you would be a good fit, please reach to me by email.

Our research spans empirics and theory: ideal candidates would have an interest in both, and some experience in one. We welcome a wide range of backgrounds: you may have an applied background in chemistry, brain imaging, computer vision or text generation, or a theoretical background in applied mathematics, statistics or optimization, or a mix as many engineers do.

How to join my group

Undergraduates and masters: a good way to work with me is to take a class I am teaching, do well in it, and then to intern with me.

PhD students: you may apply to the PhD program in ECE at UW-Madison, list my name as someone you would like to work with, and write me an email as early as possible to present yourself. It is not required but helps to have worked with me beforehand.
